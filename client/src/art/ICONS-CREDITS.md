# Icon credits

The game's icons (`client/src/art/icons.ts`) are, except where noted below, from **game-icons.net**:

> Icons by **Lorc, Delapouite & contributors**, https://game-icons.net, licensed under
> [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

They come from the npm package `@iconify-json/game-icons` (v1.2.4, a devDependency). `scripts/build-icons.mjs` copies
only the icons listed here into `client/src/art/icons.gen.ts`.

**Changes:** coordinates are rounded to 0.1 unit on the 512 grid, and `move` is rotated. The icons are recoloured at
runtime (`currentColor`) and can carry an offset shadow. The shapes are otherwise unchanged.
